# Signal Atlas

Signal Atlas treats information as a navigable field rather than a set of rooms. Every message, shipment change, risk movement, decision, task, and milestone is a coordinate in the same living terrain. The composition should feel discovered in motion: a fine timeline spine, compact annotations, and recurring node forms create a visual grammar that lets the eye travel without asking the reader to translate between modules. Text is sparse and exact, used as an instrument label, never as a wall of explanation.

Chronology is the primary rhythm. Quiet intervals are intentional breathing room; consequential moments interrupt them with a clear rail, a denser card, or a precisely placed marker. The page is cool white and materially calm, while the geometry carries the energy: 8-point intervals, patient alignments, hairline rules, and small metadata create the feeling of a meticulously calibrated field notebook. Density must feel composed, not crowded. Every row earns its place through hierarchy and timing.

Colour is a disciplined classification language. Purple marks the active system and the route into depth. Emerald, amber, red, and blue are not decoration or mood; they are semantic rails that repeat with unwavering consistency so a glance can separate healthy, attention, critical, and informational signals. Saturation is reserved for the moment of meaning. The palette should feel painstakingly tuned against the white ground, with the restraint of a master instrument maker.

Depth is context-preserving. A drawer, peek card, or overlay should open like a transparent layer over the field, keeping the originating signal visible behind it. Relationships become legible through connections, halos, and bands: suppliers, shipments, and buyers are not isolated lists but a constellation with visible pressure points. The atlas is an invitation to move from a single signal to its surrounding system without losing the thread.

The final expression is editorial precision made operational: a small phone frame carrying an enormous amount of considered thought. Repeated marks, carefully held margins, and typographic restraint should make the work feel laboured over for countless hours by someone at the top of their field. Nothing is ornamental without purpose; nothing is accidental. The calm comes from craftsmanship — from painstaking attention to scale, contrast, sequence, and the quiet confidence that the field will remain intelligible even when the work is urgent.

## Visual register

- **Canvas:** 375 × 812 pt phone frame, 16 pt outer margin, 8 pt rhythm, safe-area top and bottom insets.
- **Type:** Inter 400 / 500 / 600; compact 11 pt metadata labels; short editorial headings.
- **Foundation:** `#FAFBFC` page, `#FFFFFF` cards, `#212833` text, `#5E687B` muted, `#E5EAF0` rules.
- **Active system:** `#9000FF` primary.
- **Semantic rails:** emerald `#16A34A` healthy, amber `#D97706` attention, red `#DC2626` critical, blue `#2563EB` informational.
